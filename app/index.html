    Shortcuts
    • Double-click text to edit it on the label; Esc finishes.
    • Drag to move, drag the corners to resize.
    • Things snap to the centre, the margins and each other; hold Alt while dragging to stop them.
    • Arrow keys nudge the selection (Shift for 1 mm).
    • R rotates, Del deletes.
    • In the Elements list, drag a row's grip, or press Alt+↑ / ↓, to bring it forward or send it back.
    • Shift-click, or drag across empty space, to select several.
    • Ctrl+A selects everything, Ctrl+D duplicates.
    • Ctrl+C and Ctrl+V copy and paste, also between labels.
    • Ctrl+B, I and U format what's selected while typing.
    • Ctrl+scroll, or + and −, zoom.

    Batch list

    Paste a list, comma-separated values, or cells from a spreadsheet, and put {1}, {2}… in your text where each value goes. Every line prints as its own label.

    {0} inserts the whole line.

    The label doesn't use the list yet, so every label would come out the same. Put {1} in a text box (or use the Field menu).
