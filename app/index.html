    Drag to move, drag corners to resize. Elements snap to the centre, margins and each other; hold Alt to turn snapping off. Del deletes, arrow keys nudge (Shift for 1 mm), R rotates. Shift-click or drag across empty space to select several. Ctrl+C / V copy and paste (also between labels), Ctrl+D duplicates, Ctrl+A selects all.
